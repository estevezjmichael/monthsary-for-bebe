To my dearest SumairaWith all my heart ♡

Happy 11th monthsary, my love.

Sixteen months ago, nakilala kita. Eleven months ago, naging tayo. I still feel so grateful that, out of all the people in this world, I got to know you and love you. I made this little gift hoping it could bring even the smallest smile to your face.

But before anything sweet, I'm truly, deeply sorry. I know that these past few weeks, paulit-ulit tayong nag-aaway, and I recognize that my actions have hurt you and disturbed your peace of mind. I'm sorry for the times I made things heavier for you, for the times I failed to listen well, and for the pain I caused. You never deserved that from someone who loves you.

I don't want to make excuses. I don't want to use this special day to rush you into feeling okay, either. Your feelings are valid, and your peace matters. You deserve to be heard, respected, understood, and treated gently—especially when things are difficult.

I know that saying sorry isn't the same as changing. I want to do better in ways you can actually feel: pausing before I react, listening without becoming defensive, choosing my words carefully, owning my mistakes, and respecting your space when you need it. Hindi ko hihingin na agad mong makalimutan lahat. I want to rebuild the trust and comfort I've affected, slowly and consistently, through my actions.

Even with the distance between us, I want my love to feel peaceful, not exhausting. I cannot promise we'll never have a hard day, but I can promise to take responsibility for how I show up in our relationship. I want to keep learning how to love you with the care you deserve.

Thank you for these 16 months of knowing each other and 11 months of being together. I love you so much, Sumaira. I'm sorry, and I'm here to do the work—not just say the words.

With love, patience, and a sincere heart,

John Michael ♡
